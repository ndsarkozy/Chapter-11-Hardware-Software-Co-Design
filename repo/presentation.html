The Problem We Solved

The original lab measured the right thing,
but you couldn't feel it.

Original Ch.11 lab

  • Run AES-128 in software, then in hardware
  • Compare two timing numbers from MQTT
  • Submit the speedup ratio
  • Student is a spectator

Our redesign

  • Wire a potentiometer + LCD + run a CPU load
  • Watch the LCD visibly lag the knob
  • Flip one #define, watch lag vanish
  • Student is a participant
